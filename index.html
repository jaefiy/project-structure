📚 What You Will Learn (Year 5 KSSR)

6.3.1 Perimeter of Composite Shapes

Perimeter is the total distance around the outside edge of a composite shape. Remember: Do not add inner shared borders!

6.3.2 Area of Composite Shapes

Total Area = Area of Shape A + Area of Shape B. We use formulas for 5 shapes:

  • Square & Rectangle: Length × Width
  • Triangles (Right-angled, Isosceles, Equilateral): ½ × Base × Height
